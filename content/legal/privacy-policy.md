{{APP_NAME}} is provided by {{COMPANY}} ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we handle when you use the {{APP_NAME}} app and the website at {{DOMAIN}}, and the choices you have.

## The short version

- {{APP_NAME}} is built so that your partner never sees which apps you pause, what you do in them, your messages or where you are. **We do not see those things either.**
- We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, your pairing, your schedule times, and the things the app itself creates, such as minutes, requests, challenges and kudos.
- We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising tools in the app.
- You can export your data and delete your account at any time, in the app.

## What we never collect

- **Which apps or websites you pause.** You choose them in Apple's Screen Time picker. Your choices are stored only on your own phone and never leave it.
- **Your Screen Time usage.** Apple keeps this on your device. The app can show it to you, privately, on your own phone. It is never sent to us or to your partner.
- **What you do inside any other app,** your messages, your browsing or your contacts.
- **Your location.**

## What we collect

### Information you give us

| Information | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Email address, and your Apple account identifier if you use Sign in with Apple | To create and secure your account and to send you service emails |
| Display name and optional profile photo | So your partner sees who you are |
| Time zone and language | To run your schedule at the right local time and show the app in your language |
| Your pairing with your partner, and invitations you send or accept | To link your two accounts |
| Your protected time schedule (days and times only) and your settings, such as your daily maximum | To run protected time and apply your choices |
| Challenges you create, notes or photos you choose to attach when you complete one, kudos messages, and reasons you add to a request | To show them to your partner, which is the purpose of these features |
| Messages you send to support | To help you |

### Information the app creates

| Information | Why we need it |
|---|---|
| Your minutes: what you earned, spent and what expired | To keep your balance correct and show it to you and your partner |
| Unlock requests and unlocks: when, for how long, by which method, and the answer | To make unlocking work and to build your shared report |
| Challenge completions, streaks and weekly reports | To show your progress together |
| Notification tokens for your device | To send you notifications |
| Purchase status: whether you have premium, which plan, and when it renews or ends | To give you and your partner access to premium |
| Product events, such as "pairing completed" or "first challenge confirmed", without any content you typed | To understand which parts of the app work and to fix problems |

### The website and the waitlist

- If you join the waitlist we store your email address, the page you signed up from and the time. We send one email asking you to confirm, and then only news about the launch.
- Our website host processes standard technical information, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver pages and protect the site.
- The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## What your partner can see

Your partner sees your display name and photo, your minutes balance and when minutes expire, requests between the two of you, challenges, kudos, your shared streak and report, and neutral status notes such as "Protected time is off on Ana's phone" or "Ana used an emergency unlock".

Your partner cannot see which apps you paused, your Screen Time usage, anything from other apps, or your location.

## How we use information

We use the information above to:

- provide the service: accounts, pairing, protected time, unlocking, minutes, challenges, kudos, reports and premium;
- send notifications and service emails, such as an unlock request from your partner or a sign-in link;
- keep the service secure and prevent misuse;
- understand how the app is used, in aggregate, and improve it;
- answer support requests; and
- meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build profiles for third parties. The weekly report email is off unless you turn it on.

### Legal bases (for people in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland)

- **Contract:** to provide the service you asked for.
- **Legitimate interests:** to keep the service secure and to improve it, in ways that do not override your rights.
- **Consent:** for optional emails such as the waitlist and the weekly report email. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Legal obligation:** where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

## How long we keep information

- **Account and app data:** for as long as you have an account.
- **When you delete your account:** you are unpaired and your personal data is removed from our systems within 30 days, as backups roll off. Records your partner needs for their own history may remain in a form that no longer identifies you.
- **When you unpair:** your history is kept in your account and active minutes are reset.
- **Waitlist emails:** until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and no longer than [period] after launch.
- **Purchase records:** as long as tax and accounting law requires.

## Your choices and rights

- **Export:** Settings → Export my data gives you a copy of your data.
- **Delete:** Settings → Delete account deletes your account. Steps are also on our support page. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your Apple subscription settings.
- **Correct:** you can change your name, photo and settings in the app.
- **Notifications and emails:** you can turn notification categories on or off in Settings, and unsubscribe from optional emails with the link in each one.
- **Unpair:** you can unpair at any time, on your own.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect and disclose, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. ## Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, access controls that limit each account to its own data and its own pair's data, and hashed storage for one-time codes. The secret used for in-person unlock codes is stored in your phone's secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you and the relevant authorities about a breach where the law requires it.

## Where information is processed

We and our service providers process information in the United States and other countries. If you use {{APP_NAME}} from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to and processed in those countries, with safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where the law requires them.
