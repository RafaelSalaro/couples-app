After a few years together it is easy to believe you already know everything about each other. You know their coffee order, their opinion of your cousin and the story about the school play. So conversation shrinks to logistics: who is picking up what, and when.

But people keep changing, and there is always more to know. Often the only thing missing is a good question, and a moment with nothing else competing for attention.

Here are 75, sorted from light to deep. You do not need to work through them. Pick one and see where it goes.

## How to ask (this matters more than the list)

- **Put the phones out of reach first.** A question asked with one eye on a screen will not go far.
- **Ask one question, not ten.** The best conversations come from following up: "What was that like?", "Tell me more", "Why that one?"
- **Answer too.** This is a conversation, not an interview. Sometimes go first.
- **Let silence happen.** A pause usually means someone is thinking.
- **Do not fix or debate the answer.** Just be curious.
- **Skip freely.** If a question does not land, laugh and choose another.
- **Try asking while doing something.** Walking, cooking and driving make talking easier than sitting face to face.

## Easy openers for any evening

1. What was the best part of your day?
2. What made you laugh today?
3. What was harder than you expected this week?
4. What are you looking forward to?
5. What is something small that made you happy recently?
6. Who did you enjoy talking to this week?
7. What is on your mind that I do not know about yet?
8. What would make tomorrow a good day?
9. What is something you learned this week?
10. If today had a title, what would it be?

## Light and playful

11. What is your most unpopular food opinion?
12. If we opened a restaurant, what would we serve?
13. Which three films could you watch any time?
14. What would your perfect lazy Sunday look like, hour by hour?
15. What song takes you straight back to being a teenager?
16. If you could be very good at one thing overnight, what would you pick?
17. What is the best meal you have ever eaten?
18. Which fictional character would you like to have dinner with?
19. What is the strangest job you would secretly enjoy?
20. If we had a free weekend and a full tank, where would we go?
21. What is a tiny thing that gives you far too much joy?
22. What would the title of your autobiography be?
23. Which of your habits do you think I find funniest?
24. What skill of mine would you borrow for a day?
25. If our home had a theme song, what would it be?

## About the two of you

26. What do you remember about the first time we met?
27. When did you first think this might be something?
28. What is one of your favorite memories of us?
29. What is something I do that makes you feel loved?
30. What is a small thing I do that you would miss if I stopped?
31. When do you feel closest to me?
32. What have we got better at as a couple?
33. What is something we used to do that you would like to do again?
34. What is the best trip or day out we have had?
35. What do you think we are really good at, as a team?
36. What is something about me that surprised you after we got together?
37. What do you think I do not know about how much you do for us?
38. What would you like more of in our evenings?
39. What is one thing I could do this week that would make your life easier?
40. What is a tradition you would like us to start?

## Looking back

41. What did you love doing when you were ten?
42. Who was your favorite teacher, and why?
43. What did your family do at weekends when you were small?
44. What is a smell that takes you back to childhood?
45. What was your first job like?
46. Which friend from your past do you still think about?
47. What is the bravest thing you have done?
48. What is something you believed as a child that turned out not to be true?
49. What advice would you give yourself at twenty?
50. What is a moment you are quietly proud of?

## Looking ahead

51. What would you like to be doing more of a year from now?
52. Where would you love to live for a year, if anything were possible?
53. What is something you want to learn?
54. What does a really good ordinary day look like for us in five years?
55. What is a place you want us to see together?
56. What would you do with a month off?
57. What is a dream you have not said out loud for a while?
58. What would you like us to save up for?
59. What is one thing you would like to be different by this time next year?
60. What do you hope we are like when we are old?

## Going a little deeper

Choose a relaxed moment for these, and let the other person pass if they want to.

61. What has been weighing on you lately?
62. When do you feel most like yourself?
63. What is something you find hard to ask for?
64. What do you need more of at the moment: rest, fun, quiet, company?
65. What is a worry you have not mentioned?
66. What does feeling supported look like for you?
67. What is something you have changed your mind about in the last few years?
68. What are you most grateful for right now?
69. Is there something you wish I asked you about more often?
70. What does a good life mean to you these days?

## Questions for the two of you to decide

71. What should we do this weekend that we will remember?
72. What is one thing we could say no to this month, to make room?
73. What would make our mornings easier?
74. What is a small adventure we could have this week?
75. When should we do this again?

## Ways to use them

**A question jar.** Write your favorites on slips of paper and keep them on the table. One a night at dinner.

**A walk and three questions.** Each choose one, then one together.

**A long drive.** Take turns choosing a number between 1 and 75.

**A weekly check-in.** Pick two from "About the two of you" and one from "Looking ahead" on Sunday evening.

**Date night.** Start with the playful ones before the food arrives, and see how far down the list you get.
