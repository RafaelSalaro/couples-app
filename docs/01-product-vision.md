# 01 — Product vision

## The problem

Couples spend evenings side by side, each scrolling their own phone. Solo screen-time apps (Opal, One Sec, ScreenZen) treat this as a personal willpower problem, and most people abandon them within weeks because nothing holds them to it. Couples apps (Paired, Cupla) build connection but do nothing about the phone itself.

## The idea

A couples app that protects time together. Partners pair their phones and agree on **protected time** (default 6 PM to 9 AM). During protected time, social media and other chosen apps are paused on both phones. Partners unlock time **together**, in three warm ways:

1. **In person:** scan your partner's rotating QR code.
2. **Ask your partner:** send a request; they say "Go for it" or "Let's wait" from a notification or email.
3. **Earned minutes:** complete shared challenges ("cook dinner together", "phone-free walk") or receive spontaneous **kudos** from your partner ("you made me laugh today") to earn minutes you can spend later.

## Central philosophy

> **This app is not about control. It is about being intentional and real connection.**

This sentence drives marketing, design and every product decision. The biggest worry a new user will have is "is this a way for my partner to control me?" The product answers that before it is asked: identical powers for both, instant unpairing, no visibility into what the other person does on their phone, and rewards rather than punishments.

## Who it is for

- **Launch audience:** couples (dating, living together, married) who feel their phones are crowding out time together and want to change that as a team.
- **Later (not v1):** friends, siblings and family pairs, and small groups syncing to one goal. The data model supports these from day one; the product launches couples-only.
- **Age:** adults. See `13-risks-and-open-questions.md` for the minimum-age decision.

## Why it can win

- **Built-in growth:** every user must invite a partner.
- **Built-in retention:** people quit solo habit apps; they are less likely to quit when someone they love is counting on them.
- **A clear story:** "Less scrolling. More us."
- **A distinctive mechanic:** partner-verified challenges and unprompted kudos turn screen time into small moments of appreciation. No competitor combines app blocking with a couples relationship loop.

## Positioning against competitors

| Competitor | What they do | How we differ |
|---|---|---|
| Opal (~$100/yr) | Solo scheduled blocking | Built for two; social accountability; cheaper per person |
| One Sec, ScreenZen | Solo friction pauses | Real protected time plus a relationship loop |
| Habit Doom | Unlock after self-reported habits | Partner verification, shared challenges, kudos |
| Brick, Foqos | Physical tag unlock | Rotating QR on the partner's phone, no hardware |
| Crayp | Friends lock phones together for a session | Ongoing daily practice for couples, earned minutes |
| Paired (~$40/yr) | Daily questions for couples | Acts on the phone itself; challenges happen offline |

## Success measures (first 6 months after launch)

- Pairing completion: at least 60% of people who install have a paired partner within 7 days.
- Week-4 retention: at least 35% of pairs still active.
- Trial to paid: at least 8% of pairs.
- Kudos per active pair per week: at least 2 (the emotional heartbeat of the product).
- Store rating: 4.6 or higher.

## Taglines (candidates)

- "Less scrolling. More us."
- "Put the phone down, together."
- "Be here, together."
- "Time back for the people who matter."
